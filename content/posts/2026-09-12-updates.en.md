# Major Infrastructure Changes

- **Sunsetting the de-he-micro-0003 node.**
    
    After 2,5 years of operation, I will be shutting down this reliable workhorse of a VPS. It is my first long running VPS and has seen a variety of workloads in its lifetime.

- **Sunsetting kairos (Legacy GoTrain)**

    Similarly, I will also be sunsetting kairos, a school project from at least a year ago. Because it runs on de-he-micro-0003 which I will be shutting down and I see no reason to spin it back up it will go along with de-he-micro-0003. I announced its deprecation nearly 3 months ago, if you require this service to be available, contact me.

- **Naming scheme change**

    In order to modernise and make management easier, I will be changing to a new naming scheme, this will mean that the following hosts will be renamed

    - de-he-medium-0001 -> ffm1-prod-web-0001
    - nl-nh-small-0002 -> ams1-prod-web-0001

    The old domains will stay, but as a CNAME to the new naming.

- **Migrated services**

    The following services previously hosted on de-he-micro-0003 will be migrated

    - *Umami*
        Previously left in a broken state, will be rehosted, monitored and placed on the service page.
    - *imgpush*
        An unmonitored "shadow" service, will be rehosted and monitored.
    - *rCC de-he proxy*


- **Other**

    There are a lot of old and unused DNS records under knijn.one., I will be cleaning this up.
    Caddy will run in a container for easier version management.
