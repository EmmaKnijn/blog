# GoTrain instability and upgrades
Yesterday (2026-10-07) GoTrain went down around 8:30AM UTC, this was triggered by an outage upstream at NS outside of our control.

  "average_messages": 0

The bigger issue came when GoTrain never recovered, it stayed down until well into the evening because it didnt receive or process any messages anymore. GoTrain required a manual restart to get going again.

In order to prevent this from happening again in the future, I have set up extra observerability for GoTrain using Grafana, with alerting. I hope to be able to respond to issues quicker this way.
